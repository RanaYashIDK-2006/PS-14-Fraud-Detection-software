# Phase 21 — Real-World Validation & Production Promotion Gate

## Executive Summary

**Legitimate real-world data does NOT exist in this environment.**

No dataset in the project meets the 10 acceptance criteria required for production model validation. The only development-eligible dataset (IBM `credit_card_transactions-ibm_v2.csv`, 24.39M rows) is SYNTHETIC — generated by the Synthetic Data Vault (SDV) — and the 2017 regime shift has been classified as a **SYNTHETIC_REGIME_ARTIFACT** (Phase 17).

Three real-world datasets were found (ULB creditcard, Kaggle fraudTrain/Test) but all are LEGACY: insufficient size, PCA-transformed or schema-mismatched, and with unverifiable label timing.

**Result: REAL_WORLD_VALIDATION=BLOCKED. PROMOTION=BLOCKED.**

The project must move to **data acquisition** rather than another modeling experiment.
